🔄 Clear Website Cache

If you're not seeing the latest updates to our wedding website, this page will help clear your browser's cache and ensure you get the most recent version.

Why might you need this?
Sometimes browsers store old versions of websites to make them load faster. This can prevent you from seeing new features like our song request form or updated wedding details.
Manual Steps (if needed):
1. Chrome/Edge: Press Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
2. Firefox: Press Ctrl+F5 (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
3. Safari: Press Cmd+Option+R
4. Or go to your browser settings and clear browsing data/cache
